POLICY DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
─────────────────────────
Over-reliant reviewers  → Restore independent judgment through FORCED PRACTICE.
                          They have learned to defer; the fix is practice reps
                          they can't skip (blind cases / seeded probes) + friction
                          that surfaces disagreement (disagreement prompts).

Under-reliant reviewers → CALIBRATE CONFIDENCE, not force more independence.
                          They already exercise independent judgment — making them
                          do more blind cases is redundant. Their cost is an
                          assisted-accuracy deficit from ignoring valid AI signals.
                          The fix is retrospective feedback showing P(AI correct |
                          confidence band): they need to learn WHEN to trust the AI,
                          not to practice doubting it more.

NOTES
─────
1. capability_estimate, interval_lo/hi, deferred_rate, committed_rate and blind_sample_n
   come from MOCK data (same schema as Person A's handoff table).  Substitute the real
   table and re-run — no policy code changes needed.

2. "blind_cases" means cases where the AI recommendation is withheld until after the
   reviewer commits a label — identical to the blind_first arm mechanism.

3. "confidence_calibration_feedback" = a weekly report showing the reviewer their
   own under_reliance events alongside the calibration curve
   (P(AI correct | confidence bucket)) from out/calibration_curve.csv.
   The goal is to show that high-confidence AI signals (≥0.80) are accurate ~92%
   of the time — reviewers who routinely override those signals are paying a
   pointless accuracy cost.

4. Stop conditions use 4-week rolling windows to avoid stopping prematurely
   due to noise.  All stop decisions should be confirmed by a human supervisor.

5. Reviewers already in the blind_first arm receive seeded_probes as their escalated
   intervention (not more blind cases, which they already have) and disagreement_prompts
   if escalation is warranted.

6. Workload rebalancing (volume reduction) is added whenever blind_sample_n < 15,
   because the capability estimate is too uncertain to rely on; reducing case volume
   while increasing probe density improves both measurement and protection.
